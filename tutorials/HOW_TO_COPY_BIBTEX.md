# How to Copy BibTeX

1. Select one bibliographic item in Zotero.
2. Click **Copy BibTeX** in the **Metadata Enricher** item-pane section, or right-click the item and choose the direct **Copy BibTeX** action.
3. Wait for the **BibTeX copied to clipboard** confirmation, then paste normally with `Cmd-V` wherever you need the BibTeX.

The plugin asks Zotero's own built-in **BibTeX export translator** to generate the text. It does not maintain a second BibTeX formatter and does not change your Quick Copy keyboard settings.

This action does not alter the Zotero item.
